# Known issues

Accepted behaviors that could otherwise be reported as findings. Anything outside these
bounds is in scope.

| Issue | Accepted behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Small Thanos balance | A lone balance can truncate to zero at high shifts; maximum loss is 0.153 ETH at the highest effective price of 61.44 ETH per whole ticket (15.36 ETH per trait entry), less than 1% of one entry. |
| WWXRP | WWXRP is a joke prize token with no backing or intended value. The vault owner, and any minter it trusts, can mint any amount to anyone and burn any balance; supply, price and balances are unprotected. That includes minting WWXRP to enter the daily draw (about 606 FLIP a day on average) and the century incinerator (10% of the BAF day's lost FLIP), whose winners are weighted by WWXRP burned: the vault owner can take nearly all of that FLIP. Findings about WWXRP value, supply or draw weighting are out of scope; a path where WWXRP moves ETH, stETH, sDGNRS or tickets, or pays FLIP beyond what those draws pay by rule, remains in scope. |
| Governance | Outcomes of valid sDGNRS governance approval are governance decisions, not findings; bypasses of authorization, voting or execution rules remain in scope. Genesis-only self-disruption is excluded. |
| Reserved RNG words | `rawFulfillRandomWords` returns without storing when the delivered word is 0 or 1 (1 is the waiting sentinel), leaving the request pending for the existing retry/rotation paths. Daily nudges are not added to the stored word: they apply only to the coinflip's word at daily apply (raw word plus the frozen nudge count, modulo 2^256), after this check; every other consumer reads the raw word. The accepted probability is 2/2^256 per delivered word; no entropy-normalizing fallback is used. Other loss of callback authority or retry liveness remains in scope. |
| Terminal unfinished games | Terminal entry kills unfinished lootbox, Degenerette and non-daily Craps entropy consumers. No prior session word is retained to resolve them. Earned claims and the terminal level's paid ticket obligations retain their ending treatment; losing those claims or tickets remains in scope. |
| Loan settlement timing | Loans due at a level settle in the first mining stage after the request that enters that level. Auto-repay reads the borrower's claimable and prepaid afking balances when that stage runs, and the level's random word can theoretically be public by then, so a borrower may add or withdraw those balances after seeing it and so choose between repayment and seizure. In practice, loans will be processed before the word is public and it is unlikely anyone would prefer to get liquidated and even more unlikely that knowing the word would change that preference. Each loan is still retired exactly once: the lender receives the full owed amount or the account. |
| Packed request ID | Callback authority is the coordinator address plus the low 64 bits of the request ID (`vrfRequestId`, packed beside `vrfCoordinator`); the request paths (daily, mid-day, retry, terminal) refuse an ID whose low 64 bits are 0, and the coordinator-swap re-issue stores such an ID as 0, which accepts no answer. A consumed session keeps its answered ID, so a coordinator that delivered the same request twice would overwrite `rngWordCurrent` while read-cohort consumers of that session (boxes, bets, Craps, Decimator) are unresolved, and later consumers would resolve on the second word. Accepted: Chainlink VRF v2.5 deletes the request commitment before it calls back, so an honest coordinator answers each request once. A superseded (retry or re-issue) or dropped request keeps no authority unless its low 64 bits equal the stored ID (2^-64 per late delivery). |
| Mid-day credit pricing | A mid-day request charges the caller's donated credit 300,000 x (max(basefee, 0.01 gwei) + 0.25 gwei) x 2 wei, using the request block's basefee. The coordinator bills the fulfilment at that later transaction's gas price, so a fulfilment priced above the basefee plus the 0.25 gwei tip allowance (an extreme priority fee or a basefee spike) can cost the subscription more than the request's credit. `donateVrf` credits a quarter of the ETH it forwards, so each credit wei is backed by four donated wei; that covers a fulfilment price of about 18 times (basefee + 0.25 gwei) at the measured callback bill of about 107,000 gas. The request is refused above the owner-set basefee ceiling (4x that ceiling for a Craps window; zero disables it). Any excess is paid from the subscription balance, which the level top-up and daily refill replenish. |
| VRF funding floors | The minimum fulfilment balance, low-water and target levels use the lane constants compiled into `ContractAddresses` (`VRF_LANE_MAX_GAS_PRICE`, 200 gwei, and `VRF_NATIVE_PREMIUM`, 24%, for the Ethereum mainnet lane), scaled by the callback gas limit above 150,000. A mid-day lootbox request needs 1.5x the minimum fulfilment balance; daily, Craps and terminal requests are not balance-gated. A coordinator swap to a lane with a higher maximum gas price or premium does not change the compiled floors, and an underfunded subscription leaves requests unanswered until it is funded (`donateVrf` or a direct subscription top-up), after which the existing retry, rotation and 14-day paths apply. |
| Hard-stall ending | Fourteen days after the last completed daily session (`_vrfDeadmanFired`), or when a request's word is not in use 14 days after it was sent (`_vrfDead`), the game always takes the deterministic dead-VRF ending, even if the coordinator would still answer: no loan settlement, no terminal request, and the terminal level's tickets share the pot through `claimDeadVrf`. Reaching it through an actor-induced stall or brick remains in scope. |
