# Known issues and accepted assumptions

The following are disclosed design/acceptance boundaries for this handoff. A mechanism
or impact outside a stated bound remains a separate review question.

| Case | Disclosed behavior / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled operation | Daily opening and on-schedule battle resolution are assumed. They are operational expectations, not ordering enforced on every permissionless entry point. |
| Daily VRF retry | Only the vault owner can fire it, once, 12 hours after a daily request whose word has not been received; it is the last resort before a governance coordinator swap. The retry re-sends the same request without re-stamping it, and replaces the request ID, so a late fulfillment of the original request is discarded. A coordinator swap re-sends the request and spends the retry, so the retry can never discard the new coordinator's first answer; a replacement that also stalls is recovered by another swap or reaches the VRF-dead ending. |
| Mid-day request promotion | A lootbox-only mid-day VRF request stalled past 4 hours is abandoned by any caller's advance and re-issued as that day's daily request; the promoted request keeps its own 12-hour retry. A coordinator swap re-issues a mid-day request in flight (lootbox-only or not) on the new coordinator, keeping its original send time. |
| Vault AFKing recovery | `DegenerusVault.recoverAfkingFunding` is permissionless: anyone can return the vault's staged AFKing salvage reserve to the vault at gas cost, which makes the vault's salvage-buyer fallback in `sellFarFutureEntries` and the AFKing-funded leg of its own daily auto-buy interruptible until the owner re-stages. No value leaves the vault. |
| Unopened craps comp window | A window-ahead comp seat on a day that never opens, including a VRF-stalled day, is not refunded by the whole-day lapse sweep, which walks day tickets only. The seat was paid from the vault's FLIP comp lane, so the loss is comp-lane FLIP, not player money. Accepted under the scheduling assumption. |
| Cross-day progressive qualification | One day marker per winner can be overwritten by another day's routine win before the earlier event resolves; this can remove its doubling. Accepted under scheduled resolution. Shared progressive-pool depletion is also resolution-order-dependent. |
| Extended unattended advance | More than 30 days without a sealed day ends the game in every phase; with VRF alive that is the normal VRF payout. The gap backfill (`_backfillGapDays`, bounded at 31 days for gas) therefore never sees a wider live gap, and on the normal ending the days beyond the bound hold no ticket or foil entry; coinflip stakes placed on them (possible only if nobody advances for over a month after the trigger) never settle, and FLIP is tombstoned at game over. Days skipped by a shorter stall receive derived words and coinflip settlement only: no daily draw, no foil board, no seal. |
| Deadman bounds every level | The 30-day no-sealed-day deadman applies at level 0 too, so the 365-day deploy window holds only while someone completes an advance at least every 30 days. A dead VRF is caught earlier, 14 days after its unanswered request. |
| Deadline rescue window | When distress buys meet the target on the deadline day after that day's seal, liveness reads true the next day from its start until that day's advance applies its word (the request and fulfilment transactions); the advance then completes the day and sets lastPurchaseDay. Inside that window purchases, burns and afking subscriptions revert, decimator claims take the terminal route, and an sDGNRS redemption claim settles in terminal shape and forfeits its FLIP escrow. The game recovers and no funds are lost; claimants can wait for the day to complete. |
| Deadline after unattended days | The purchase deadline fires only at the start of a caught-up day, before that day has a word. On the first day after the deadline the trigger therefore reads true until an advance runs, with the effects listed under the deadline rescue window (purchases, burns and afking subscriptions revert, decimator claims take the terminal route). If nobody advances that day, it reads false again from the next day: the next advance catches up instead, credits the skipped days to the deadline like stall days, purchases reopen, and it finishes its own day normally; the ending starts at the first advance of the following day, drawing every cohort bought up to then. A target met in that stretch rescues the level. |
| Day dropped at a deadman ending | The normal ending always draws on a terminal word it requests itself after liveness froze entry. If the 30-day deadman ends a game while a daily request from before the freeze is in flight or its day is stuck in processing (a backlog, or a stage that cannot finish), that request's word only finishes materializing the cohort it committed. The day is then dropped: its daily jackpot never pays and those funds stay in the terminal pot; its coinflips settle on its own word if it was applied, otherwise on a word derived from the terminal word. |
| VRF-dead ending | A daily VRF request with no word delivered 14 days after its send (retries and coordinator swaps do not restart the window) ends the game deterministically with no entropy. An unanswered mid-day request normally promotes on the next daily advance; if a terminal ending or the no-seal deadman blocks that promotion, it reaches the same deterministic ending 14 days after its original send. It is judged at the advance by delivery: a daily word that was delivered, applied or not, means VRF works, even one delivered after the window before any advance latched the ending, so a game stuck processing that day ends by the deadman on a fresh terminal word instead. Early-level deity refunds apply; no terminal affiliate share. The pot is split across every ticket of the terminal level: each uncreated entry (queued, or in an undrained foil pack) takes the average pot / total, and the created tickets share the rest equally per non-empty trait, then equally within each trait. Claims (`claimDeadVrf`) stay open until the 30-day final sweep. A pending sDGNRS gambling-burn pool resolves at 100%. Pending lootboxes, degenerette bets and coinflips waiting on a word are not settled. |
| Whale-pass boon timing | A lootbox-rolled whale pass queues its tickets at `claimWhalePass`, so the queued levels follow the level at claim, not at box open; the claim window is four days from the roll. |
| Decimator bounty pricing | A decimator box's ETH-target bounty converts to FLIP at the active ticket level (`_activeTicketLevel`) when the batch settles, so a level advance between burn and settlement changes the FLIP paid. |
| Lootbox boon resolution | The boon draw is keyed on the live level when a box is opened, not the level at purchase or at the RNG request. The boon weight table is fixed, but its budget normalization is level-priced, so the same committed word can deliver a different boon type or hit chance if the box opens before versus after a level advance. Opening is permissionless and swept by the crank; the order of an open against an advance is not enforced. Accepted under the scheduling assumption. |
| Nested-DGNRS ordering unproven | `test/repro/LootboxNestedDgnrsOrdering.t.sol`'s nested-spin case is `vm.skip`ped as of the single-symbol Degenerette change. Its `RNG_WORD` was hand-picked so a nested ETH spin won DGNRS under the old per-gold-count tables and spin derivation; under the shared table and the new player/result draws that word yields two batches instead of three. Both parent DGNRS wins still pay in full and the pool stays solvent, but with no nested child win the fresh-versus-stale pool pricing check has nothing to distinguish. A scan of words 1..1199 found no replacement. This is LOST COVERAGE, not a passing result: the ordering property is unproven until a new word is pinned. |
| Degenerette resolution | Assumes roughly chronological resolution; capped bonus allocation can depend on resolution order. |
| Small Thanos balance | A lone balance can truncate to zero on division at high shifts. The accepted bound is under 0.64 whole tickets (about 0.153 ETH at the highest ticket price), confined to the small position. |
| Affiliate rounding/selection | Deterministic affiliate selection and bounded floor-of-sum rounding differences; not a general allowance for redirecting funds or arbitrary rounding loss. |
| Bulk whale commission | The fresh affiliate rate halves at five or more paid passes in one call. A buyer can keep the full rate on the remainder above a multiple of five by buying it in a separate call; bonus passes are counted per five within a single call, so the split cannot gain a bonus pass. Accepted. |
| Vault-owner bounty eligibility | The DGVE-majority wallet is always bounty-eligible, regardless of activity or time of day. |
| Owner comps | Vault-owner comps charge no delegate allowance; their only cap is the shared FLIP comp lane balance. Recipients are unrestricted. |
| Governance | Outcomes requiring valid sDGNRS governance approval are governance decisions, not findings. Bypasses of authorization, voting or execution rules remain in scope. Genesis-only self-disruption is excluded. |

## Token and integration semantics

- sDGNRS and GNRUS are soulbound, not conventional transferable ERC-20s.
- DGNRS is a transferable ERC-20 whose only destination restriction is a revert on transfers
  to the DGNRS contract itself.
- FLIP protocol-authorized spending, coinflip auto-claim and special VAULT/sDGNRS routing
  are intentional; wallet supply, virtual allowance and backing are not interchangeable.
